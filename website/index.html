📖 Step-by-Step Guide

📍 Select Your Course – Choose from over 400+ golf courses across the globe.

🏌️ Take Your Shot – Swing with style and power!

📲 Record Your Shot – Tap the record button to log your stroke. Optionally change your club.

🚶 Walk to Your Ball – Update your club and lie as needed.

🔄 Record Next Shot – Press record again to track your next stroke.

🏁 Finish the Hole – Tap “Finish Hole” to move to the next one.

🔁 Repeat for All Holes – Continue the process for every hole on the course.

🏆 Finish Your Round – Tap “Finish Round” to save your scorecard.

📊 View Stats & Analysis – Go to the Stats section to see your performance.

😄 Happy Golfing! – Enjoy your game and keep improving!

Sprint Goals!

The project was divided into 3 sprints

Sprint 1

The goal from this sprint was to have a basic app with a working GPS and functionality to gather golf stroke data.

  • Basic app: React Native / Expo
  • Map view: Focus map on gold courses. Display hole and user position. Show strokes. Cycle through holes.
  • Golf course DB: collect a library of golf courses.
  • Scorecard: register score and display. Calculate summary.
Sprint 2

In this sprint we needed to extend the app to include more functions and visuals for the user

  • Course search: smart search for avaliable courses based on distance from your location.
  • History view: display completed rounds with all details.Display distances and strokes gained for each shot made.
  • Settings tab: adjustable settings.
  • Statistics: calculate important statistics for a given round such as tee, approach, chip, putt.
Sprint 3

More details and fancy stuff for golf players.

  • Club selection: tailored club selection for each give shot. Update your personal bag with your favourite selection of clubs.
  • Lie selection: record your shots' lie and get statistics based on your performance.
  • Detailed statistics: show detailed statistics in a new neat graph format, and added new statistics page for each golf club
  • Onboarding: informative slides to get you started fast!
